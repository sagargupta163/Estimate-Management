Table design: Estimation

Column NameDatatypeDescription
estimated_idint (PK)Unique identifier of the estimation. Auto-numbered here.
chain_idint (FK)References chain_id from the chain table. Links the estimation with the chain.
group_namevarchar(50)Stores the group name.
brand_namevarchar(50)Stores the brand name.
zone_namevarchar(50)Stores the zone name.
servicevarchar(100)Stores the service details.
qtyintStores the total quantity or units of that service.
cost_per_unitfloatStores the cost of each unit.
total_costfloatStores the estimated amount (qty × cost_per_unit).
delivery_datedateStores the delivery date.
delivery_detailsvarchar(100)Stores the delivery details.
created_atdatetimeDate and time the estimation was first created.
updated_atdatetimeDate and time the estimation was last updated.
